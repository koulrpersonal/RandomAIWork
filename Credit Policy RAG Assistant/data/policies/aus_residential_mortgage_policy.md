# APEX BANK AUSTRALIA — RETAIL CREDIT RISK POLICY MANUAL
## Document ID: POL-AU-MORTGAGE-2026.1
**Effective Date:** 01 January 2026  
**Supervisory Standard:** APRA Prudential Practice Guide CPG 223 / NCCP Act Responsible Lending  
**Jurisdiction:** Australia (Commonwealth)

---

## SECTION 1: SECURITY ASSESSMENT & MAXIMUM LOAN-TO-VALUE RATIO (LVR)

### [Clause 1.1] Standard Base LVR Cut-Offs
1. **Owner-Occupied (Principal & Interest):** Maximum standard Loan-to-Value Ratio (LVR) is **80.00%** without requiring Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI).
2. **Investment Properties (Principal & Interest):** Maximum standard LVR is **80.00%** without LMI.
3. **Interest-Only Terms:** Maximum standard LVR is capped at **70.00%** for owner-occupied and **80.00%** for investment loans. Interest-only period may not exceed 5 consecutive years.

### [Clause 1.2] Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) & High-LVR Tiers
1. Applications with LVR between **80.01% and 95.00%** (inclusive of capitalised LMI premium) require primary underwriting approval from the bank's designated LMI provider (Genworth/Helia or QBE).
2. Maximum gross LVR cannot exceed **95.00%** under any circumstance.
3. First Home Buyer Guarantee Scheme (FHO) applicants may borrow up to **95.00% LVR** without LMI premium subject to federal government eligibility certificate.

### [Clause 1.3] Property Security Restrictions & High-Density Postcodes
1. **Minimum Living Area:** Studio and one-bedroom apartments must have a minimum internal floor area of **40 square metres** (excluding balcony, car space, and storage cages).
2. **Small Units (40m² to 49m²):** Maximum allowable LVR is capped at **70.00%**.
3. **High-Density Inner City Postcodes:** In designated Sydney (2000, 2010), Melbourne (3000, 3004), and Brisbane (4000) high-density apartment precincts, maximum LVR is capped at **75.00%** with a maximum development concentration limit of 15% of units in a single strata complex.

### [Clause 1.4] Cash-Out and Equity Release Policy
1. Cash-out for personal investment, renovations, or liquidity reserves up to **$100,000 AUD** requires signed statutory declaration of loan purpose.
2. Cash-out exceeding **$100,000 AUD** requires verified documentary evidence (e.g., formal builder contract, council approved plans, or invoice quotes).
3. Unacceptable cash-out purposes include: business restructures, crypto-asset purchases, gambling, or debt consolidation exceeding 3 credit accounts.

## SECTION 2: APPLICANT INCOME VERIFICATION & SHADING POLICY

### [Clause 2.1] Base PAYG Salary and Wages
1. Base gross salary for permanent full-time and permanent part-time applicants is recognized at **100.00%** of current gross pay.
2. Minimum verification requirement: Two most recent consecutive computer-generated payslips showing year-to-date (YTD) earnings plus 3 months bank statements showing automated payroll credits.
3. Applicants within probation periods are eligible only if probation is within the same industry with minimum 2 years continuous prior employment.

### [Clause 2.2] Overtime, Shift Allowances, and Discretionary Bonuses
1. **Overtime & Shift Penalties:** Recognized at a maximum shaded rate of **80.00%** of the annualized amount. Requires evidence of consistency over the last 12 to 24 months.
2. **Annual Discretionary Bonuses:** Recognized at a maximum shaded rate of **80.00%** calculated as the average of the last 2 consecutive financial years. If the most recent year is lower by >20%, the lower figure must be used.
3. One-off sign-on bonuses, relocation allowances, and discretionary director allowances are **0% recognized**.

### [Clause 2.3] Casual and Fixed-Term Contract Employment
1. **Casual Employees:** Minimum continuous tenure with current employer is **6 months** (or 12 months across same industry). Income recognized at **80.00%** of lowest annualised run-rate.
2. **Independent Contractors (IT / Professional Services):** Must hold minimum 12 months track record in contracting. Income shaded at **85.00%** if contracting through an Australian registered company (Pty Ltd) or ABN.

### [Clause 2.4] Self-Employed Borrowers (Sole Trader, Partnership, Company, Trust)
1. Minimum operating history of **2 full financial years** under current ABN/ACN.
2. Documentation: Last 2 consecutive years of personal tax returns, company/trust tax returns, financial statements (Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet), and ATO Notices of Assessment (NOA).
3. **Income Calculation:** Calculated as the 2-year average of net profit before tax plus allowable add-backs (depreciation, interest on refinance debt, non-recurring expenses).
4. If Year 2 profit dropped by more than **20.00%** compared to Year 1, Year 2 net profit must be used as the benchmark income.

### [Clause 2.5] Residential and Commercial Rental Income
1. **Existing Residential Rental Income:** Shaded at **80.00%** of gross market rent to buffer for vacancy periods, council rates, insurance, and managing agent fees.
2. **Proposed Rental Property (Purchase):** Shaded at **80.00%** of rental valuation determined by bank-appointed certified valuer.
3. Short-term holiday stays (e.g., Airbnb / Stayz): Shaded at **60.00%** with minimum 12 months verified ATO tax return history.

## SECTION 3: SERVICING CAPACITY & RESPONSIBLE LENDING BENCHMARKS

### [Clause 3.1] Assessment Interest Rate Floor and Buffer
1. In accordance with APRA Prudential Guidance, serviceability must be calculated using the higher of:
   * Current customer contract interest rate plus an **interest rate buffer of 3.00% p.a.**
   * The bank's internal floor assessment interest rate of **7.50% p.a.**
2. For interest-only loans, repayments must be amortized over the remaining Principal & Interest term after the interest-only period expires.

### [Clause 3.2] Living Expenses and Household Expenditure Measure (HEM)
1. Living expenses must be captured using the bank's 14 standard expenditure categories in compliance with ASIC Regulatory Guide RG 209.
2. Declared borrower living expenses must be compared against the benchmark **Household Expenditure Measure (HEM)** corresponding to applicant marital status, dependants, and postcode.
3. The **higher of declared living expenses or the HEM benchmark floor** must be utilized in all serviceability debt service calculations.

### [Clause 3.3] Debt-to-Income (DTI) and Net Serviceability Ratio (NSR)
1. **Standard Maximum DTI:** Total aggregate gross customer debt across all credit providers divided by total gross annual income must not exceed **6.00x**.
2. **High DTI Classification:** DTI between **6.01x and 7.00x** is classified as High Risk and requires Level 2 Delegated Lending Authority (DLA) approval and non-HEM expense verification.
3. **Absolute DTI Cap:** Debt-to-Income exceeding **7.00x** is strictly declined and cannot be approved under policy.
4. **Net Serviceability Ratio (NSR):** Minimum acceptable NSR is **1.05x** (or Uncommitted Monthly Income / UMI of $150 AUD per adult).

## SECTION 4: GENUINE SAVINGS & COMPREHENSIVE CREDIT REPORTING (CCR)

### [Clause 4.1] Genuine Savings Rule
1. For any home loan where LVR exceeds **80.00%**, the borrower must demonstrate a minimum of **5.00% genuine savings** held in the applicant's name for at least **3 continuous months**.
2. Acceptable genuine savings include: term deposits, shares held >3 months, verified continuous rent paid through licensed real estate agent for >6 months, or equity in existing real estate.
3. Unacceptable genuine savings: First homeowner grants, gifts from family, proceeds from personal unsecured loans, or developer rebates.

### [Clause 4.2] Comprehensive Credit Reporting (CCR) & Default Thresholds
1. **Clean Credit Requirement:** All applicants must have a clear credit file with Equifax / Illion bureau score of >= 600.
2. **Paid Defaults:** Minor paid telco/utility defaults under **$500 AUD** are acceptable without policy exception if settled >12 months ago.
3. **Unpaid Defaults:** Any open/unpaid default, court judgment, or pending litigation constitutes an automatic decline.
4. **Hard Inquiries:** More than 4 consumer credit inquiries within the preceding 6 months requires credit officer scrutiny for undisclosed debt.

## SECTION 5: DELEGATED LENDING AUTHORITY (DLA) & EXCEPTION MATRIX

### [Clause 5.1] Approval Authorities Matrix

| Approval Authority Tier | Role / Committee | Approval Scope & Exception Limits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Standard Credit Officer** | Credit Assessor | • Loans up to **$1,500,000 AUD**<br>• Max LVR: **80.00%**<br>• Max DTI: **5.50x**<br>• Fully conforming policy only (Zero exceptions) |
| **Tier 2: Senior Credit Manager** | Senior Risk Underwriter | • Loans up to **$3,000,000 AUD**<br>• Max LVR: **90.00%** with LMI<br>• DTI: **5.51x to 6.50x**<br>• Shading exceptions (e.g. 18-month self-employed history) |
| **Tier 3: Credit Committee / CRO** | Head of Underwriting / Chief Risk Officer | • Loans exceeding **$3,000,000 AUD**<br>• LVR: **90.01% to 95.00%**<br>• DTI: **6.51x to 7.00x**<br>• Studio apartments < 45m²<br>• Postcode concentration exception |

### [Clause 5.2] Compensating Risk Factors for Exceptions
When a Tier 2 or Tier 3 exception is submitted, the underwriter must document at least **two** of the following compensating factors:
1. Significant net liquid assets post-settlement exceeding 12 months mortgage payments.
2. Historical continuous employment tenure exceeding 5 years with same tier-1 employer.
3. Low LVR (<= 60.00%) mitigating loan servicing risks.
4. Strong family guarantor security (parental equity guarantee capped at 20% LVR).
